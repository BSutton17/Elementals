Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Elementals is a browser game. This page explains exactly what it stores about you, why, and how to get rid of it. It describes what the software actually does — not what it might do one day.

You can play the entire game without an account. If you never sign in, nothing about you is stored on our servers at all — no account, no statistics, no identifiers. Every kingdom, every mode and every match is available to you.

1. If you play as a guest

We store nothing about you on our servers. Your browser keeps two small values on your own device, which never reach us:

  • A tutorial flag — remembers that you have opened “How to Play”, so it stops nudging you.
  • A per-tab session id — lets you reconnect to a match you are already in if your connection drops. It is discarded when you close the tab.

The name you type before a match is sent to the game server so other players can see it during that match. It is not written to any database and is gone when the match ends.

2. If you create an account

What we receive from Google

Signing in uses Google. Google confirms who you are and gives us three things: a permanent Google account identifier, your email address, and your Google display name and profile picture.

We keep only the identifier and the email. Your Google display name and profile picture are used once, to suggest a username, and are never stored. We never receive your Google password.

What we store

What Why
Google account identifier To recognise you when you sign in again. It never changes.
Email address Account recovery and support. We do not send marketing email.
Username The name other players see. Chosen by you.
Level and experience Progression.
Coins earned and spent An in-game currency with no real-world value. Kept as a list of changes so a balance can be checked and corrected.
Match results For each match you play: which kingdom, where you placed, how long you lasted, damage dealt and taken, gold earned and spent, abilities used, and eliminations. This produces your record.
Daily quest progress To track the three quests each day.
Cosmetic items Which skins you own and which kingdom wears each one.
Account dates When the account was created and last used.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not log or store IP addresses.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. There are no third-party trackers, pixels or advertising cookies on this site.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. The only things stored in your browser are the items listed in this policy, and they are used to run the game.
  • We do not sell anything for real money. Coins are earned by playing.

4. Children

You must be 13 or older to create an account. We ask your date of birth when you sign up and do not create accounts for anyone younger.

Anyone of any age can play as a guest, with the full game and nothing stored.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

5. Who else handles your data

These companies operate parts of the service on our behalf:

  • Google — sign-in. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Supabase — the database, hosted in the United States (US East).
  • Heroku (Salesforce) — the game server.
  • Netlify — hosting for this website.

Your data is stored in the United States. If you are in the UK or EU, this means it is transferred outside your region.

6. How long we keep it

Account information is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, email, username and progression are permanently removed.

Match records are kept, with your identity removed. The row that says “a Fire kingdom placed third in a seven-player match” stays, because it is how the game is balanced — but the link to you is erased, so it is no longer information about a person.

7. Your rights

You can do all of these yourself, from your profile page in the game — no need to ask us:

  • See your data — your profile shows your record and progression.
  • Download your data — export everything we hold as a file.
  • Correct it — change your username at any time.
  • Delete it — delete your account permanently.

If you are in the UK or EU, the GDPR gives you these rights and the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California, the CCPA gives you similar rights. We honour all of them regardless of where you live.

8. Security

Sign-in is handled by Google, so we never see or store your password. Connections to the game use encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure, and we hold as little about you as the game can function with — which is the most effective protection available.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the game before it takes effect.
